## Sheet 5. Finish with a one-page decision

1. **Goal:** Write the job, amount under consideration and horizon.
2. **Evidence:** Record two checked criteria with their source IDs.
3. **Arithmetic:** Show one calculation with inputs and units.
4. **Status:** Chosen, shortlisted or waiting. Explain why.
5. **Review:** Name the event or new evidence that would reopen the decision.

**Optional AI prompt**

> Use only my labeled excerpts to compare the two funds for my stated goal. Keep dates, units and fee labels attached to each figure. Show inputs and steps for any calculation. Mark unsupported fields as unresolved. Identify the missing evidence most likely to change my decision.
